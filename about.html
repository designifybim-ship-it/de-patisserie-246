The heart behind De' Patisserie.

Hi, I’m Daneisha Forde. My love for food started from childhood, learning through real kitchen moments and growing into a passion for creating treats that make people smile. Baking gives me a way to express care, creativity and joy all at once.

As I got older, I studied Home Economics, continued my culinary education and gained experience working in the industry. After an injury changed the way I could work, I chose to build something of my own. De' Patisserie became the space where I could keep doing what I love while creating at a pace that works for me.

Every order means something to me. I want people to feel the love, sweetness and intention behind what I make, whether it is a single treat box, a dessert order or a Saturday drop built just for fun.
