Before you order.

A few quick notes so your order goes smoothly.

50%deposit is required after your order is approved. Your order is confirmed once the deposit is received.
Order ahead

Spots are limited each week. Cakes, cheesecakes, cupcakes and cookie cakes need at least 3 days' notice, and two-tier cakes need 1 week.

Order approval

Your order is pending until De' Patisserie confirms it on WhatsApp.

Payment methods

CIBC First Pay, bank transfer and cash are available. Payment details are confirmed after approval.

Pickup and delivery

Choose pickup or delivery when ordering. The final time and any delivery fee are confirmed after we receive your order.

Dietary requests

Add any allergies or dietary needs to your order notes. De' Patisserie will confirm what can be accommodated.
