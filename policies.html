Good to know.

Limited availability

De' Patisserie is run alongside a full-time job, so only a small number of orders can be taken each week. Please order as early as you can, as dates fill up quickly.

Order ahead

Cakes, cheesecakes, cupcakes, cookie cakes and decorated cookies need at least 3 days' notice. Two-tier cakes need at least 1 week. Other bakes need at least 1 day.

Pickup and delivery times

The time you choose on the order form is a preference. We'll confirm a pickup or delivery time that fits our schedule on WhatsApp before your order is final.

Rush orders

Orders inside the notice period can only be taken if a slot is free, and a $50.00 rush fee applies. Please message us first to check.

Saturday Treats

Saturday Treats is paused for now. Message us on WhatsApp to be added to the list, and we'll let you know when the next drop opens.

Cinnamon roll boxes

When Saturday Treats is open, the minimum order is 2 rolls. Boxes come in 2, 4 or 6, in one flavour or mixed, and you can add up to 3 boxes to one order.

Payment methods

CIBC First Pay, bank transfer and cash are available. Payment details are confirmed after approval.

Pickup and delivery

Choose pickup or delivery when ordering. Delivery fees go by parish, from $5.00 to $25.00, and are confirmed after we receive your order.

Dietary requests

Add any allergies or dietary needs to your order notes. De' Patisserie will confirm what can be accommodated.
