Policies and order notes.

Please read these notes before placing a Saturday Treats order.

Order approval

Your order is pending until De' Patisserie confirms it on WhatsApp.

Deposit

A 50% deposit is required after your order is approved. Your order is confirmed once the deposit is received.

Payment methods

CIBC First Pay, bank transfer and cash are available. Payment details are confirmed after approval.

Pickup and delivery

Choose pickup or delivery when ordering. The final time and any delivery fee are confirmed after your ticket is received.

Dietary requests

Add any allergies or dietary needs to your order notes. De' Patisserie will confirm what can be accommodated.

Limited flavours

Flavours can change from week to week. Unavailable flavours are marked on the menu.

Saturday Treats is De' Patisserie's weekly cinnamon roll drop. Send your order ticket on WhatsApp for approval.
